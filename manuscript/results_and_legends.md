# Results

### Lipodystrophy alters the hypothalamic transcriptome, predominantly in males

To test whether chronic hypoleptinaemia affects the hypothalamus in generalized lipodystrophy, we performed RNA sequencing of hypothalamic tissue from lipodystrophic *Ldlr*−/−;aP2-nSrebp1c-Tg mice (LD) treated with saline or metreleptin for [duration], and from non-lipodystrophic *Ldlr*−/− controls (WT) (n = [n] per group and sex). Compared with WT, saline-treated LD mice had 456 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) in males (137 up, 319 down) but only 62 in females (28 up, 34 down) (Fig. 1a). Metreleptin-treated LD mice differed from WT at 297 genes in males and at 4 genes in females (Fig. 1a).

### Metreleptin normalizes a hypothalamic energy-deficit signature

Saline-treated LD males showed the arcuate signature of energy deficit: expression of the orexigenic neuropeptides *Agrp* (log₂FC = 1.88, adjusted P = 1.9 × 10⁻⁶) and *Npy* (log₂FC = 1.07, adjusted P = 0.024) was increased (Fig. 1b). Alongside these changes we found:

- induction of glucocorticoid-responsive genes (*Fkbp5*, *Zbtb16*, *Cdkn1a*, *Sult1a1*, *Plin4*, *Hif3a*; log₂FC 1.02–1.55);
- induction of the cold-inducible RNA-binding proteins *Rbm3* and *Cirbp*;
- an endothelial activation program (*Vwf*, *Icam1*, *Serpine1*, *Pecam1*, *Nos3*, *Klf4*, *Osmr*, *Tgm2*; log₂FC 0.44–1.49) (Fig. 1b).

None of these 18 genes differed between metreleptin-treated LD males and WT controls. *Icam1* and *Tgm2* were also significantly lower in metreleptin-treated than in saline-treated LD mice (Fig. 1b). Metreleptin-treated LD males had increased expression of the canonical leptin target *Socs3* (log₂FC = 0.99) and the leptin-responsive arcuate transcription factor *Nhlh2* (log₂FC = 0.62), confirming hypothalamic leptin signalling (Fig. 1b). In females, *Agrp* and *Npy* were not differentially expressed. However, *Rbm3* and *Vwf* were increased in saline-treated LD females, and metreleptin significantly corrected 11 genes that were altered in saline-treated LD females, including *Vgf*, *Podxl*, *Plxnd1*, *Nr2f2*, *Htra1* and *C4b*.

### In males, an oligodendrocyte and myelin deficit persists despite metreleptin

In parallel, saline-treated LD males showed coordinated downregulation of the oligodendrocyte lineage (log₂FC −0.30 to −1.03; Fig. 2a), spanning:

- lineage transcription factors (*Olig1*, *Olig2*, *Sox10*, *Myrf*);
- premyelinating oligodendrocyte markers (*Bcas1*, *Enpp6*, *Gpr17*);
- structural myelin components (*Mbp*, *Plp1*, *Mog*, *Mag*, *Mobp*, *Cnp*, *Cldn11*, *Opalin*);
- myelin lipid synthesis (*Ugt8a*, *Fa2h*, *Aspa*);
- glial gap junctions (*Gjc2*, *Gjb1*, *Gjc3*).

Astrocyte genes (*Aqp4*, *Il33*, *Fabp7*, *Car2*, *Gpr37l1*) were also reduced. Unlike the energy-deficit signature, the myelin deficit was not corrected by metreleptin. In metreleptin-treated LD males, 23 of the 25 oligodendrocyte and myelin genes remained decreased relative to WT, with effect sizes nearly identical to those in saline-treated mice (median log₂FC −0.51 versus −0.55). None of these genes differed between saline- and metreleptin-treated LD mice (Fig. 2a). Across all 169 genes differentially expressed in both comparisons, the changes in metreleptin-treated LD males closely matched those in saline-treated LD males (Pearson r = 0.96), and every gene changed in the same direction (Fig. 2b). In females, a subset of these genes (*Olig1*, *Bcas1*, *Mog*, *Mal*, *Cldn11*, *Qdpr*, *Gjc3*) was modestly reduced in saline-treated LD mice (log₂FC −0.23 to −0.36); none differed between metreleptin-treated LD females and WT (Fig. 2a).

Thus, metreleptin corrects the hypothalamic energy-deficit state of lipodystrophic mice but not the associated oligodendrocyte and myelin deficit.

# Key messages (slide version)

1. Lipodystrophy remodels the hypothalamic transcriptome, mainly in males (456 vs 62 DEGs).
2. The energy-deficit state is leptin-reversible. Metreleptin normalizes *Agrp*/*Npy* and the glucocorticoid-response, cold-inducible and endothelial programs. *Socs3* and *Nhlh2* confirm hypothalamic target engagement.
3. In males, the glial deficit is leptin-resistant. The coordinated loss of the oligodendrocyte and myelin program persists under metreleptin (r = 0.96).
4. Implication: leptin replacement may not protect hypothalamic white matter in lipodystrophy.
